# Changelog

## 0.1.0 — 2026-11-09

- **First release.** The expected output of a PV module plane from the MeteoSwiss local forecast: global and diffuse radiation and air temperature, hour by hour for nine days, read from the open data with range requests. Sensors for the energy of today, tomorrow, the day after and the rest of today, the power of the current and the next hour, today's peak and its time; the forecast feeds the energy dashboard's solar production forecast. Setup from the Home Assistant location or a place/postal-code search, with peak power, azimuth, tilt, losses, inverter limit and a damping factor for calibration. Reconfigure moves the plant; Configure changes the plane. Repairs notice when the data cannot be read, diagnostics download, German, English, French and Italian.
