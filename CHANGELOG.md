# Changelog

## 0.1.0 — 2026-11-09

- **First release.** The expected output of a PV module plane from the MeteoSwiss local forecast: global and diffuse radiation and air temperature, hour by hour for nine days, read from the open data with range requests. Sensors for the energy of today, tomorrow, the day after and the rest of today, the power of the current and the next hour, today's peak and its time; the forecast feeds the energy dashboard's solar production forecast. Setup from the map marker or a street address: the roof planes of the building from the Sonnendach solar cadastre (SFOE) with orientation, tilt, area, suitability and long-term yield, picked from a list and corrected if needed, or a plane entered by hand; peak power, losses, inverter limit and a damping factor for calibration; the forecast point is chosen automatically. With a cadastre roof, sensors for the typical day of the month and the forecast as a percentage of it. Reconfigure moves the plant; Configure changes the plane. Repairs notice when the data cannot be read, diagnostics download, German, English, French and Italian.
